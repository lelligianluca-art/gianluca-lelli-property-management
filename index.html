What is the Investment Index?

The Investment Index is a virtual product I developed to answer a simple but critical question: which Italian city offers the best opportunity for a real estate investment right now?

It combines market data, tourism demand, short-term rental performance indicators, purchase prices, regulatory environment and yield potential into a clear ranking and analysis of Italian cities.

Whether you are considering Rome, Florence, Milan, Bologna, Naples, Bari or emerging destinations, the Index helps you make a more informed decision before you buy.

What the Index evaluates

  • Rental yield potential — expected short-term and long-term returns
  • Tourism & demand — visitor flows, seasonality and occupancy trends
  • Purchase price levels — entry cost vs. expected income
  • Regulatory environment — local rules for short-term rentals
  • Growth outlook — infrastructure, connectivity and market momentum

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreign investors buy apartments in Italy for short-term rentals?

Yes. Non-residents can purchase residential property in Italy and must comply with CIN/CIR and local rules. A local partner simplifies the process.

What returns can I expect from a short-term rental apartment in Rome?

Returns depend on location, size and management. Well-located apartments can achieve attractive occupancy and rates versus long-term leases.

Do I need to live in Italy to run a short-term rental?

No. Many foreign owners operate remotely with a trusted local manager.

What is CIN and CIR registration?

Mandatory identification codes for short-term rental properties in Italy, required to list legally on Airbnb and Booking.com.
